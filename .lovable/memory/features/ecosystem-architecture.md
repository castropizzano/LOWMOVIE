The project is structured in 3 distinct intellectual layers, each with its own circulation logic and licensing:

1. **LowMovie™** — the WORK. Film + dissertation + DOI. Status: consolidated. License: © all rights reserved.
2. **Lowbyrinth™** — the METHOD. Implicated research-creation framework formulated in the dissertation. Status: being formalized. License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Citable independently from the dissertation.
3. **Low Runner™** — the PLATFORM. Replicable navigable-dissertation system (this portal's codebase). Status: expanding. License: MIT.

Materialized in:
- `/ecossistema` — page articulating the 3 layers with relational SVG diagram
- `/licenca` — hybrid licensing policy (per layer)
- `/replicar` — Low Runner™ branded page (URL kept for backwards compatibility)
- `/metodologia` — includes "How to cite this method" block (ABNT) for Lowbyrinth™ as independent citable method

All 3 trademarks (™) are in INPI formalization process. The public use of ™ indicates active claim.

**Why:** strategic positioning that strengthens the defense narrative (delivers an ecosystem, not just a dissertation), creates legal coherence for future commercialization/doctoral expansion, and clearly separates what circulates open vs. what stays protected.
